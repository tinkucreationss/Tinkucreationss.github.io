About Me & TinkuCreationss

About Me

Hello! My name is Vishnu. I am the creator of TinkuCreationss. I am passionate about mobile video editing, creative design, and digital content creation.

I started exploring video editing because I enjoyed creating creative edits and experimenting with different editing ideas. Over time, this interest developed into TinkuCreationss, where I can share my editing projects and resources with other creators.

About My Channel

TinkuCreationss is my creative platform where I share video-editing related content, editing ideas, project previews, and resources for creators.

The website and my social-media channels work together: the videos show the editing ideas and project previews, while this website provides the related projects and resources in an organized way.

How TinkuCreationss Works

  1. I create and edit mobile video projects.
  2. I prepare a project preview so creators can see the editing style before opening it.
  3. I publish projects with codes such as TC001, TC002, TC003, and so on.
  4. Where a project requires specific fonts or resources, the relevant links are provided with the project.
  5. I regularly add new projects so creators can discover more editing ideas and resources.
  6. I share project previews and editing content through my YouTube and Instagram channels.

Our Goal

My goal is to build TinkuCreationss into a useful platform for creators who enjoy mobile video editing and want to discover new editing projects and ideas.

How to Use TinkuCreationss

Follow these simple steps to find a project, open it in Alight Motion, and download any required fonts.

1. Find your project

  1. Scroll through the project cards on the Home page.
  2. Use the search bar to find a project by its code (for example, TC022) or by its title.
  3. Check the project preview and title to make sure you have selected the edit you want.

2. Open the project

  1. Tap the Project button on the project card.
  2. A popup will show the available project and font links.
  3. Tap Project in the popup. Your device may ask you to open the link with Alight Motion.
  4. Choose Alight Motion and follow the prompts in the app to import or open the project.

3. Download required fonts

  1. If font links are shown in the popup, download the fonts you need.
  2. Follow the font installation or import instructions provided by your device and editing app.
  3. Reopen the project in Alight Motion and check that the text looks correct. If a font is not supplied, the project may use a substitute font.

4. If the project does not open

  • Make sure Alight Motion is installed and updated.
  • Check your internet connection and try the Project link again.
  • Some projects may require a compatible Alight Motion version or features that vary by device.
  • If the link still does not work, contact TinkuCreationss through the official social channels.

5. Stay connected

Follow TinkuCreationss for new project uploads, editing tutorials, and updates.
